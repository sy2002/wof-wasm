Every claim the chapter makes, one line each, with its source, in the order of the chapter. A claim without a source goes to the list at the end and stays out of the draft. A general fact about the machine or the world that the notes do not state is sourced in a reference work and marked **(reference)**; what the game does always comes from the notes, the listing and the port's sources. The port and the instruments are gathered at the chapter's end, as the opening says; no "How we know" box and no "What went wrong" box (`book/BOOK.md` 4, point 6; the controller's task).

Counts are of the branch's base, `4be0d77`, counted in the files named, by reading or with the command in the counts table at the end. Run for a fact, each on one core: the headless original over the script `oil_d` with its pokes (9 seconds), which kept the run's schedule of VBlanks; the native library replaying that schedule with the enemy aircraft records read after every VBlank (under a minute); `extract_tables.Image` for the small tables of the executable; `ppkc.parse` for the shapes' names, sizes and hotspots. No test run, no browser, no rebuild of the native library.

Words used in the sheet as the chapter will use them. An **enemy aircraft record** is one of the four records of `aircraft_records`; its **state** is the record's word at `+0x00`, and the oracle's random inputs are "random settings of the registered state", as chapters 5, 14 and 15 say. Its **mode** is the word at `+0x02`, its **relation** the word at `+0x04`, its **order** the word at `+0x0C`, each named with its noun. The **hit flag** is the word at `+0x06`; a ship's **hits** are its `+0x0C`, the hits it can still take; "a hit on the aircraft" is the targets' or the fighters' fire on the player (chapter 15). A **row of the sinking** is a row of pixels a ship is drawn lower; a **row of the window** is one of the 3-D view's eleven rows. "Shell" is the port's JavaScript shell in this book (the glossary's Shell, chapter 23), so a ship's gun "takes a shot" and its "splash" lands; the noun "shell" is not used. **Height** is world y, pixels above the water line; **east** is +1, the larger world x. A **pass** and a **tick** are chapter 7's.

## 1. Four records

4. The enemy aircraft live in `aircraft_records` (`0x02522A`): four records of `0x34` bytes, 52. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The record"; `src/records.def` (`WOF_RECORD(aircraft, 0x34)`); `re/Wings.lst` `0x01E7E4` (`muls.w #$34`) and `0x01E8AA` (`cmp.w #$4, d4`).
5. Every launch takes one of the four, so no more than four enemy aircraft fly at once; the record holds what a flight needs from tick to tick: where it is against the player, how fast it goes and wants to go, the height it flies towards, its health and timers. *(The "why a record": what the fields hold, read from the table; no intent claimed.)* Source: claim 4; `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The record" (the table); `src/enemy.c`, `wof_aircraft_launch` (no free record, no launch).
6. The state word (`+0x00`): 0 free, 2 flying, 4 shot down and falling, `0x10` burning on land; 1 and 8 are cases of `enemy_aircraft_step` that nothing sets. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The record" and "Open"; `src/enemy.c`, `wof_enemy_aircraft_step`.
7. The mode word (`+0x02`): 1 a fighter cruising, 2 a fighter on the player's tail, 4 a torpedo plane, `0x10` a torpedo plane that has dropped its torpedo; bit 3 set while it turns (9, `0x0A`, `0x0C`, `0x18`). The mode's value is a set of bits: bit 0 the cruise, bit 1 the tail, bit 2 the torpedo plane, bit 4 the drop, bit 3 the turn; `aircraft_fly` dispatches on the mode with bit 3 taken off. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The record"; `src/enemy.c`, `aircraft_fly` (`switch (a->mode & ~8)`).
8. The relation word (`+0x04`), worked out every tick for every record in use: 1 the same way behind the player, 3 the same way ahead of him, 2 the other way east of him, 4 the other way west of him; `+0x28` the distance. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The record"; `src/enemy.c`, `aircraft_relation` (`0x01D3B4`).
9. The order word (`+0x0C`): the place among the aircraft behind the player the same way, 1 the nearest. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The record"; `src/enemy.c`, `aircraft_order` (`0x01D476`).
10. Health `+0x08`, `0xF0` at the launch; the burst `+0x0A`, the ticks of hits still to make a burst, 5 to 7 at the launch and 6 to 9 after each; the hit flag `+0x06`, set by the guns and cleared by the flight, which starts an evasion. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The record"; `src/enemy.c`, `wof_aircraft_launch` (`wof_rand_mod(3) + 5`); `src/player.c`, `wof_guns` (`wof_rand_mod(4) + 6`).
11. Timers: `+0x10` ticks to the next turn, `0x226` or `0x113` when set; `+0x18` ticks to a turn, a hit sets 8 to 13. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The record".
12. Speed `+0x1C` in hundredths of a pixel a tick, eased each tick while the aircraft flies (state 2) towards the wanted speed `+0x1E`: speeding up by half the gap plus 5; slowing by half the gap less 5 (`sub.w $1e(a6),d0`, `ext.l`, `divs.w #2`, `subq.w #5`, `sub.w d0,$1c(a0)` at `0x01E6FC`–`0x01E708`), which leaves it 5 above the half-way point; both held between 900 and `0xA28`, 2,600. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The record"; `src/enemy.c`, `aircraft_speed` (`0x01E64E`); `re/Wings.lst` `0x01E6FC`–`0x01E708` (review A1).
13. The height `+0x26` moves towards the wanted height `+0x24` each tick by a step from `climb_steps` (`0x0261F8`) chosen by the twentieths of the distance, at most five, plus a draw of 0 or 1. Source: `src/enemy.c`, `aircraft_motion` (`0x01D796`); `re/notes/porting-m6.md`, "The tick: what part 2 ports".
14. The x moves by the speed's hundreds times `attitude_factor` by the attitude, in the ROM's fast floating point (chapter 14's table and arithmetic). Source: `src/enemy.c`, `aircraft_motion` (`wof_image32(0x025B0C + 4 * attitude)`, `wof_ffp_mul`); `re/notes/ffp.md`; `book/docs/part-2/player.md` (the attitude's factor).
15. Only two fields are written in a pass, the flash count `+0x2C` and the drawing's copy of the map offset `+0x2E`; everything else is the tick's. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The record" (observed, `tools/m6_observe.py fields`).
16. `enemy_aircraft_step` (`0x01E7D6`) walks the four records every tick; for each in use the relation and the order, then by the state: 2 its flight by its mode (`aircraft_fly`, which first trails smoke by its damage), 4 the fall, `0x10` the burning, 1 and 8 nothing; then the speed, which `aircraft_speed` eases only for state 2 (it returns otherwise), the motion unless burning, and the frame. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "What each state and mode does"; `src/enemy.c`, `wof_enemy_aircraft_step`, `aircraft_speed` (`if (a->state != 2) return;`); `re/Wings.lst` `0x01E7D6`–`0x01E8B6` (review B9).
17. The compiler turned the switch on the state into a chain of subtractions: `subq.l #1` (state 1), `subq.l #1` (2), `subq.l #2` (4), `subq.l #4` (8) and `subq.l #8` (`0x10`), each followed by a `beq` to its case; what is left over goes to the default. Source: `re/Wings.lst` `0x01E86A`–`0x01E87E` (`subq.l #$1`, `subq.l #$1`, `subq.l #$2`, `subq.l #$4`, `subq.l #$8`). *(Reading of the listing, marked.)*
18. Listing pair: `enemy_aircraft_step` from `0x01E86A` to `0x01E8B6` (kind `asm`, as `enemy_aircraft_step_states`) beside `wof_enemy_aircraft_step` (kind `c`, `src/enemy.c`).

## 2. The flights

19. A fighter cruising (mode 1, `fighter_cruise`, `0x01D9C6`) goes by its relation. Behind the player the same way: within `0xA0` pixels and first in the order it goes onto his tail, mode 2; within `0xA0` otherwise it takes his height and his airspeed less `0x46` a place; farther back, level, it closes at his airspeed plus the distance, `0x50` more for the first and less for the others. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "What each state and mode does"; `src/enemy.c`, `fighter_cruise`.
20. Coming the other way: east of him (relation 2) it slows to 900 while he turns, else it turns; west of him (relation 4) it slows to 900, takes a height `0x20` above or below his, times its turn and turns. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "What each state and mode does"; `src/enemy.c`, `fighter_cruise` cases 2 and 4 (review B7).
21. Ahead of him the same way it wants his airspeed less a quarter of the distance (`0x46` more for every other fighter ahead before it), jinks between heights of `0x23` and `0x5F`, 35 and 95 (a draw of `0x23` plus 0 to 60 in tens), and turns after `0x226` ticks, more than `0x600` pixels ahead, when he turns, or 8 to 13 ticks after the guns hit it. Source: the same; `src/enemy.c`, `fighter_cruise` case 3 (`wof_rand_mod(7) * 10 + 0x23`, `wof_rand_mod(6) + 8`).
22. A fighter on the player's tail (mode 2, `fighter_attack`, `0x01DCCC`) keeps about `0x82` pixels, 130, behind at his airspeed plus or less `0x32`, 50, flies at his height, and turns when he does (mode 9); when he is no longer ahead of it the same way it goes back to mode 1. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "What each state and mode does"; `src/enemy.c`, `fighter_attack`.
23. It fires (`+0x12`) while it is first in the order, level (attitude 0), within `0xA0` pixels and less than 8 of height, the player in the air and ahead of it the same way. Source: `re/notes/enemy.md`, the same; `src/enemy.c`, `fighter_attack`; `re/Wings.lst` `0x01DDDA`–`0x01DE30`.
24. Source: `src/enemy.c`, `fighter_attack` (`wof_rand_mod(0x20)`, `wof_rand_mod(5) + 6`); `re/Wings.lst` `0x01DE36`–`0x01DE92` (`cheat_no_damage`, `tick_parity`, `subq.w #$8, $12(a0)`); `book/docs/part-2/weapons.md` (the hit count).

A torpedo plane (mode 4, `torpedo_plane`, `0x01DEA4`) flies at the carrier at 9 pixels a tick, its launch speed of 900 hundredths, at a height of `0x32`, 50; within `0x3E8`, 1,000 pixels, before the carrier's deck it goes down to `0x14`, 20, and there, once level at that height and still short of the deck, it drops its torpedo into the sixteenth object record (chapter 15), becomes mode `0x10` and sets the enemy's countdown to 500; the torpedo runs on in the sea into the carrier. ## 4. Shot down

## Terms and glossary entries

Checked against `book/docs/glossary.md`: none of these has an entry. New, in bold with an entry, "First met and defined in chapter 16." (the build's rule: first met is the lowest chapter that bolds or links the entry; chapters 11, 12 and 14 name the 3-D window and the enemy's countdown in plain text, which the build does not count):

- Enemy aircraft record: one of four records of 52 bytes in which an enemy fighter or torpedo plane flies, its state saying free, flying, falling or burning. The detail: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The record".
- Mode (of an enemy aircraft): the word of an enemy aircraft record that says what it is doing, a fighter cruising or on the player's tail, a torpedo plane before or after its drop, with a bit for a turn. The detail: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "What each state and mode does".
- Relation (to the player): the word of an enemy aircraft record, worked out every tick, that says where it is against the player: behind or ahead of him the same way, or the other way east or west of him. The detail: the same.
- Fighter: an enemy aircraft that hunts the player's aircraft: it closes from behind, gets on his tail and fires. The detail: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "What each state and mode does". Elsewhere: Wikipedia, "Fighter aircraft".
- Torpedo plane: an enemy aircraft that flies at the player's carrier and drops a torpedo at it. The detail: the same. Elsewhere: Wikipedia, "Torpedo bomber".
- Enemy's countdown: the field of the player's record that counts the ticks to the next torpedo plane. The detail: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "What launches one".
- Wreck's word: the word a wreck burnt out on land leaves in a list of forty, its x, negative facing west, by which the pass draws it. The detail: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "Shot down, and what it scores".
- Ship record: one of five records of 30 bytes, the four enemy ships' and the carrier's, with its guns, its hits left, its deck's height, its score and its sinking. The detail: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "The ships".
- Gun list: dropped at the controller's ask; Ship record's line holds the guns' entries.
- Hit count (added at the review, C23): the word of the player's record that the targets' fire and the enemy fighters count down; at its end the oil falls and the fuel with it, and a new count is drawn. The detail: `re/notes/porting-m5.md`, "The targets' fire"; `re/notes/enemy.md`, "What each state and mode does". "First met and defined in chapter 16" by the build's rule (chapter 15 names it unbolded).
- Airfield (added at the review, C23): a runway on an island, marked by two records of the map (`0x114` and `0x115`, `airfields_scan`), from which enemy fighters take off one at a time while the player is near; the map's table gives each its parked aircraft and its most up. The detail: `re/notes/enemy.md`, "What launches one" and "The fifteen maps". "First met and defined in chapter 16" (chapter 13 names airfields unlinked).
- 3-D view: the window in the dashboard's middle that shows the sky, the sea and the map ahead of the aircraft as from the cockpit, with the enemy aircraft ahead in it. The detail: `re/notes/porting-m4.md`, "The pass"; `re/notes/porting-m6.md`, "The pass: what part 1 ports".
- Enemy plane counter: the dashboard's count of the enemy aircraft shot down, two digits and a kill icon a plane. The detail: `re/notes/porting-m6.md`, "The pass: what part 1 ports".

Not made terms: the order, the burst, the hit flag, the firing word, the turn, the kill icon, the sinking, the arrow, the cruise ship, the Japanese carrier, jinking (glossed where met).
