Every claim the chapter makes, one line each, with its source, in the order of the chapter. A claim without a source goes to the list at the end and stays out of the draft. No test run, no browser, no rebuild of the native library.

Words used in the sheet as the chapter will use them. The **rank** is the campaign's, `rank_played` (`0x0253BE`), 0 to 6; the high-score file's word is "the rank field". The **mission number** is `mission_number` (`0x0253C0`), the mission within the rank, from 1. An **entry** is a byte of the demo's buffer; a high-score file's 36 bytes are a **row**; a menu's line is a menu's **item**. A **flag** is a game's byte or word of yes and no (the won flag, `balloons_on`, `night_flag`); the walker's fourth argument is its **mode**, 0 or 1, so that "flag" keeps one sense. The saved game's eight parts are its **pieces**, each **direct** (the memory at the address) or **pointed** (the block whose address is stored there), the walker's two kinds; "record" stays a map record, an object record, a ship record. A saved game is "a saved game" or "the file"; "save" is the verb only, and "the save script" is the name of `save_a`; "saving" is the act. "Entry" is a demo byte only; the high scores "ask for a name"; a sort's round is a "sweep", never a pass. A **pass** and a **tick** are chapter 7's; a **round** of a menu is one VBlank (`re/notes/frontend.md`, "The timetable, left alone": each round one `graphics.WaitTOF`).
