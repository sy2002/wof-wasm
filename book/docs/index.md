# Bringing Back Wings of Fury

A 1990 Amiga game ported to the browser, and how we know it is faithful

A book about how a 1990 Amiga game was ported, routine by routine, to the browser, and how one knows the port is faithful; then what is inside the game and how it uses the Amiga; then the code, the tools and how to build it. The game itself is [one page further](play.md), playable.

## Preface

Why this port exists and why it is told: retro preservation, a case study in how such a port can be made today, and who did what, the owner and the AI sessions, in the open.

## How to read this book

Who the book is written for, the order of its three parts and the ways through them, and what the sidebars, the listings and the figures are: every figure rendered from the game's data and every listing extracted from the real sources when the site is built.
