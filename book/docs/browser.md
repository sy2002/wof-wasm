# The shape browser

Every shape of the game's twelve [shape containers](glossary.md#shape-container), 1,049 in all, is here as the disk holds it, drawn from the files by this book's build in the colours the game shows its container in, colour 0 left as the ground, as [chapter 12's contact sheets](part-2/shapes.md#the-contact-sheets) draw them.

/// html | div.wof-browser
The shape browser is drawn by a small script of this site from data its build made, and needs JavaScript.
///

Choose a container, or find a shape by the name the program asks for, and pick it to read its record's header as [chapter 12](part-2/shapes.md#the-header-field-by-field) takes it apart: the size and the [hotspot](glossary.md#hotspot), the stored planes and their [plane masks](glossary.md#plane-mask), the [clear and set bytes](glossary.md#clear-and-set-bytes), whether the blit gives it a mask or draws it [opaque](part-2/shapes.md#the-blit-exactly), and for the player's aircraft and its weapons [the mirror](part-2/shapes.md#the-mirror). The colours are the numbers the planes store, not what the clear and set bytes make of them on the screen. The shapes are the game's art: they belong to the authors and publisher of Wings of Fury, Broderbund (1990), and are kept here for preservation without any right to them being granted.
